2. Daten, die nur auf deinem Gerät gespeichert werden

Die App speichert die folgenden Angaben ausschließlich im App-Speicher deines Geräts:

  • Receiver: Adresse, Anschlüsse, Name, Modell und Benutzername deiner Receiver. Passwörter liegen verschlüsselt im sicheren Speicher von Android (Android Keystore).
  • IPTV-Listen und Anbieter: Adressen deiner Wiedergabelisten und Zugangsdaten deines Anbieters, verschlüsselt im sicheren Speicher. Listen, die du vom Gerät öffnest, legt die App als Kopie im App-Speicher ab.
  • Einstellungen und Nutzung: Einstellungen, gespeicherte Ansichten, zuletzt gesehene Sender, Wiedergabepositionen in Aufnahmen und Filmen sowie die Sehstatistik für „★ Meistgesehen“ (Sender und Sehdauer).
  • Zwischenspeicher: Senderlogos, Programmdaten und Vorschaubilder, damit die App schneller lädt.
  • Protokoll: Abläufe und Fehlermeldungen der App, zum Beispiel Gerätemodell, Android-Version, Adressen deiner Receiver und Sendernamen. Zugangsdaten in Internetadressen macht die App im Protokoll unkenntlich. Das Protokoll ist auf wenige Megabyte begrenzt; ältere Einträge werden überschrieben.
  • Pro: ob Pro freigeschaltet ist.
  • Mitschnitte und Screenshots (Pro): Wenn du sie startest, speichert die App sie in den Ordnern „Movies/Enigma2 Player“ und „Pictures/Enigma2 Player“ deines Geräts.

Diese Daten erhält der Entwickler nicht. Du kannst sie jederzeit löschen: in den Android-Einstellungen unter Apps › Enigma2 Player › Speicher › „Speicher leeren“ oder durch Deinstallieren der App. Mitschnitte und Screenshots bleiben in deinen Medienordnern, bis du sie selbst löschst.

Dabei erhält der jeweilige Server technisch bedingt deine IP-Adresse und gegebenenfalls deine Zugangsdaten. Für diese Server gelten die Datenschutzbestimmungen ihrer Betreiber.

Zahlungsdaten gibst du nur bei Google ein; die App erfährt lediglich, ob Pro gekauft wurde, und bestätigt den Kauf bei Google Play. 2. Data stored only on your device

The app stores the following information exclusively in its private storage on your device:

  • Receivers: address, ports, name, model and user name of your receivers. Passwords are kept encrypted in Android’s secure storage (Android Keystore).
  • IPTV lists and providers: addresses of your playlists and your provider’s login details, encrypted in secure storage. Lists you open from your device are stored as a copy in the app’s storage.
  • Settings and usage: settings, saved views, recently watched channels, playback positions in recordings and movies, and the viewing statistics for “★ Most watched” (channels and viewing time).
  • Cache: channel logos, programme data and preview images so that the app loads faster.
  • Log: the app’s activity and error messages, for example device model, Android version, addresses of your receivers and channel names. Login details in web addresses are masked in the log. The log is limited to a few megabytes; older entries are overwritten.
  • Pro: whether Pro is unlocked.
  • Recordings and screenshots (Pro): when you start them, the app saves them in the “Movies/Enigma2 Player” and “Pictures/Enigma2 Player” folders on your device.

The developer does not receive this data. You can delete it at any time: in Android settings under Apps › Enigma2 Player › Storage › “Clear storage”, or by uninstalling the app. Recordings and screenshots remain in your media folders until you delete them yourself.

3. Connections made by the app

  • Your receiver: the app connects to your receiver’s web interface (OpenWebif or the Dreambox web interface) and its streams – using the address and login details you enter. This is your own device.
  • Search in your home network: only when you tap “Search” does the app contact devices in your local network to find receivers and their names. These requests do not leave your home network.
  • IPTV lists, streams and providers: the app retrieves the lists, streams, programme data and logos that you enter or open yourself. For technical reasons, the respective server receives your IP address and, where applicable, your login details. The privacy policies of their operators apply to these servers.
  • Google Play: the app uses Google Play’s billing system for the Pro purchase. You only enter payment details with Google; the app only learns whether Pro has been purchased and confirms the purchase with Google Play. Google’s privacy policy applies to processing by Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Demo mode: the demo receiver runs entirely inside the app on your device.
  • Other apps: opening the web interface in a browser, emailing the developer and sharing the log open other apps – only when you choose to.

Independently of the app, Google may collect crash and diagnostic data through Android and Google Play under its own terms if you have allowed this on your device.

4. Contact and log by email

If you write to the developer or send them the log via “Share”, they receive your email address, your message and, where applicable, the log. Emails are received via Gmail (Google). The developer uses this information only to handle your request, does not pass it on and deletes it as soon as it is no longer needed for that purpose. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (requests about the app) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering requests).

5. Android backup

If you have turned on Android backup, Android may back up the app’s settings to your Google account. The app excludes passwords, login details, IPTV lists and the log from this backup. You control the backup in Android settings; the developer has no access to it.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network status: for connections to your receiver, streams and Google Play.
  • Notifications and foreground service: if a recording continues while you leave the app, Android shows a notification with “Stop recording”.
  • Keep device awake: so that the screen and playback do not switch off while you watch TV.
  • Google Play billing: for the Pro purchase.

The app does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or access to all files. It only opens files and folders through Android’s file picker when you select them.

7. Sharing of data

The developer does not receive any data through the app and therefore does not pass any on. They do not sell data or use it for advertising.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). To exercise them, contact the developer by email. As the app does not transmit any data to the developer, this usually only concerns emails you have sent them.

10. Changes
